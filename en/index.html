About us

Three generations above the sea

It all began about eighty years ago with grandfather Eugenio Lagomarsino, who grew orchids on a plot by the sea between Quinto and Nervi and sold them at the flower market.

Today his son Antonio, known to everyone as Tonino, and his grandson Eugenio run the Nursery. His granddaughter Giulia looks after the shops in Nervi, Albaro and Recco.

In our greenhouses on Via dei Floricoltori we grow potted plants, mostly flowering ones, and sell them retail and wholesale. You can see the sea from the greenhouses: the view alone is worth the trip up.
