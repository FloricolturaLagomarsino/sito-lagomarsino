What data we process

Browsing data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). Like any web server, it automatically records technical data such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the service running and secure. We do not use this data to identify you.

When you contact us. If you message us on WhatsApp, call us or send us an email, we use your name, number or address and the content of your message only to reply to you and to prepare a quote, an order or a delivery. WhatsApp messages pass through WhatsApp (Meta) servers, under their terms. We keep this data for as long as needed to handle your request and, if there is a sale, for the periods required by tax law.

Legal basis. Replying to your requests and handling orders (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)); running the site and keeping it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)); optional external content (your consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
